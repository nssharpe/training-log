Warmup & Training Cheatsheet

From the MFTK Training Cheatsheet. Use the same tempo as the program for all warmup sets.

Prepare for your training

  • Make it comfortable. Add padding. Wear shoes if needed.
  • Prepare equipment. Use support. Grippy surface.

Your final warmup set should feel close to the actual training intensity. Rest the full time given in your program — usually 60s+.

Training, step by step

  1. Warm up the exercise.
  2. Complete the training set.
  3. Record your result. Use a tracking system that's simple and easy to use.
  4. Rest as long as the program says (or longer). Actually rest — do not train other stuff in between.
  5. Repeat for the number of sets, then warm up the next exercise.
Really try to beat last session.
Flexibility → progress in range of motion.
Strength → progress in weight.
